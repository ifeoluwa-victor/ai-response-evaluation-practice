# Practice Examples, Part 2 (Examples 6 to 10)

Scores are in this order: Accuracy / Relevance / Completeness / Instructions.

## Example 6: Summarising a passage
**Passage:** "A school library in the town was closed for two months for repairs. During this time, students borrowed books from a classroom shelf. The library reopened with new tables and more power sockets for laptops."

**Question:** Summarise the passage in one sentence.

**Answer A:** The school library closed for two months and reopened at a cost of five million naira with new computers.

**Answer B:** The school library closed for two months for repairs and reopened with new tables and more power sockets.

**My scores:**
- A: 2/5/3/5 = 15
- B: 5/5/4/5 = 19

**Preferred:** B

**Reason:** Answer A invents a cost and "new computers", so Accuracy is 2. It also leaves out the new tables and sockets, a key point, so Completeness is 3. Answer B is accurate and only skips the classroom shelf, a minor detail.

## Example 7: Summarising a passage
**Passage:** "A market trader says sales fell this month because heavy rain kept customers away. She plans to sell through a phone-based group chat until the weather improves."

**Question:** Summarise the passage in two sentences.

**Answer A:** Sales fell this month because heavy rain kept customers away. The trader plans to sell through a group chat until the weather improves.

**Answer B:** Sales fell this month because of heavy rain.

**My scores:**
- A: 5/5/5/5 = 20
- B: 5/5/3/3 = 16

**Preferred:** A

**Reason:** Answer B is accurate but leaves out the trader's plan, a notable point, so Completeness is 3. It also uses one sentence instead of two, which ignores one clear instruction, so Instructions is 3.

## Example 8: Spotting an unsupported claim
**Passage:** "The new footbridge across the river was built by local workers and is 40 metres long."

**Question:** Using only the passage, in what year was the bridge opened?

**Answer A:** The bridge was opened in 1998.

**Answer B:** The passage does not say what year the bridge was opened.

**My scores:**
- A: 1/5/5/3 = 14
- B: 5/5/5/5 = 20

**Preferred:** B

**Reason:** The year is not in the passage. Answer A invents it, so Accuracy is 1, and it ignores the "using only the passage" rule, which is one clear instruction ignored. Saying the text does not give the answer is the correct response.

## Example 9: Healthcare education
**Question:** Does an ultrasound scan use ionising radiation?

**Answer A:** No. Ultrasound uses high-frequency sound waves, not ionising radiation such as X-rays.

**Answer B:** Yes, ultrasound uses a low dose of X-rays to build the picture.

**My scores:**
- A: 5/5/5/5 = 20
- B: 1/5/5/5 = 16

**Preferred:** A

**Reason:** Ultrasound is based on sound waves. X-ray and CT use ionising radiation. Answer B states the wrong physics with confidence, so only Accuracy is lowered.

## Example 10: Healthcare education
**Question:** What does ALARA mean in radiography?

**Answer A:** ALARA means no radiation should ever be used on pregnant patients.

**Answer B:** ALARA means "as low as reasonably achievable". Radiation dose should be kept as low as reasonably possible while still getting the image needed for diagnosis.

**My scores:**
- A: 1/5/5/5 = 16
- B: 5/5/4/5 = 19

**Preferred:** B

**Reason:** Answer A gives a wrong meaning and turns it into an absolute rule. Pregnancy calls for careful justification and local protocols, not a blanket ban. Answer B gives the correct meaning, though it does not mention the practical methods of time, distance and shielding, so Completeness is 4.
