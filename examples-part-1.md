# Practice Examples, Part 1 (Examples 1 to 5)

Each example has a question, two options (A and B), my scores, and my reason. Scores are in this order: Accuracy / Relevance / Completeness / Instructions.

---
## Example 1: General knowledge
**Question:** What is the capital of Australia?

**Answer A:** The capital of Australia is Canberra.

**Answer B:** The capital of Australia is Sydney, its largest city.

**My scores:**
- A: 5/5/5/5 = 20
- B: 1/4/2/5 = 12

**Preferred:** A

**Reason:** Answer B gives the largest city, not the capital. It sounds confident but is wrong. The question had no format instruction, so Instructions is 5 for both.

## Example 2: General knowledge
**Question:** Why is the sky blue in the daytime?

**Answer A:** The sky is blue because it reflects the colour of the oceans.

**Answer B:** Sunlight scatters off gas molecules in the atmosphere. Shorter blue wavelengths scatter more than longer red ones, so blue light reaches our eyes from all directions.

**My scores:**
- A: 1/4/2/5 = 12
- B: 5/5/4/5 = 19

**Preferred:** B

**Reason:** Answer B gives the accepted explanation (scattering of light). Answer A repeats a common myth.

## Example 3: General knowledge
**Question:** Which planet is closest to the Sun, and about how long is its year in Earth days?

**Answer A:** Mercury is closest. Its year is about 88 Earth days.

**Answer B:** Venus is closest. Its year is about 225 Earth days.

**My scores:**
- A: 5/5/5/5 = 20
- B: 1/3/3/5 = 12

**Preferred:** A

**Reason:** Answer B names the wrong planet, so its year length is also wrong for the planet the question asked about. The 225 days is correct for Venus, but a correct side detail does not fix a wrong main answer.

## Example 4: Following a format instruction
**Question:** List three renewable energy sources as a numbered list, one line each, with no explanations.

**Answer A:** Here are four sources: 1. Solar, which uses sunlight to make electricity. 2. Wind, which uses moving air. 3. Hydropower, which uses flowing water. 4. Geothermal, which uses heat from the earth.

**Answer B:**
1. Solar
2. Wind
3. Hydropower

**My scores:**
- A: 5/4/5/2 = 16
- B: 5/5/5/5 = 20

**Preferred:** B

**Reason:** Answer A is factually correct and covers everything the question needs. But it breaks three instructions: it gives four items instead of three, it does not keep one line each, and it adds explanations. It still gives a numbered list of renewable sources, so Instructions is 2 ("ignores most"), not 1.

## Example 5: Following a format instruction
**Question:** In exactly two sentences, explain why exercise is good for health.

**Answer A:** Regular exercise strengthens the heart and muscles and helps control body weight. It also improves mood and sleep for many people.

**Answer B:** Exercise is good for you. It strengthens the heart. It also helps mood and sleep.

**My scores:**
- A: 5/5/4/5 = 19
- B: 5/5/4/3 = 17

**Preferred:** A

**Reason:** Answer B uses three sentences instead of exactly two. That is one clear instruction ignored while the rest are followed, so Instructions is 3. The content is accurate.
